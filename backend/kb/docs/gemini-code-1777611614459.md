## 1. How to Register in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I register for a new account on Suvit?
* What are the steps to create a Suvit profile?
* How do I sign up?

**Answer:**
To register a new account on Suvit, follow these steps:
1. **For Registration:** Visit the Suvit Website.
2. **Enter Mobile Number:** Enter your mobile number and click on **GET OTP**. You will receive an OTP on your number. Enter the OTP and click on **Sign In**.
3. **Fill Personal Information:** Enter your First Name, Last Name, and select your Profession to create your profile. Click **Next**.
4. **Organization Information:** Enter the number of employees or staff and indicate how you found out about Suvit. Click **Next**.
5. **Set Your Password:** Enter your E-Mail ID and set a secure password (minimum 8 characters). Click **Next**.
6. **Welcome to Suvit:** Your registration is now successful!

## 2. Verify your E-mail ID & Mobile Number in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I verify my email and mobile number?
* Why do I need to verify my profile details?
* I didn't get the OTP for email verification.

**Answer:**
Keeping your email ID and mobile number verified is crucial for a smooth profile setup and ensures your account works seamlessly. To verify your details:
1. Go to Suvit.io and log in to your account.
2. **Edit Your Profile:** Once logged in, click on **My Profile**, then click on **Verify**.
3. **Verify Your Details:** Enter the OTP sent to your contact details and click on the **Verify** button. 
*(Note: If you didn't receive the OTP for your email, please check your spam folder.)*

Once verified, you are ready for the next step: installing the Tally Connector Desktop application.

## 3. Install Suvit Desktop (Tally Connector) Application
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I download and install the Suvit Tally Connector?
* Where can I find the 32-bit Suvit desktop application?
* How does Suvit connect with Tally?

**Answer:**
The Suvit Connector acts as a bridge between your Tally software and Suvit, ensuring seamless data processing. By default, the 64-bit application will download automatically. If you require the 32-bit application, please email `support@suvit.io` to receive the download link.

**Installation Steps:**
1. **Download Connector:** Log in to Suvit. Go to **Help & Resources** and click on **Download**.
2. **Installation:** Double-click the downloaded application file. Click "Run" if prompted and wait for the installation to complete.
3. **Log In:** Open the application and enter your Suvit Username (Mobile number or E-mail ID) and Password.

*Note: Follow any on-screen instructions to configure your Tally with Suvit after a successful login.*

## 4. Subscribe to the Company and Synchronize the Ledgers
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I sync my Tally data with Suvit?
* How do I add my Tally company to the Suvit desktop app?
* Why aren't my ledgers showing up in Suvit?

**Answer:**
To process your data, your Tally company needs to be subscribed in Suvit, and its ledgers must be synced. This automates transferring data like Bank Statements, Sales, Purchases, Journal Entries, and Bulk Party/Stock Item Creations.

**Steps to Subscribe and Sync:**
1. **Open the Company in Tally:** Open the company you want to work with in Tally and make sure it is actively selected.
2. **Add Company:** Open the Suvit Desktop Application on your computer and navigate to **Add Companies**.
3. **Subscribe Company:** Find your company in the list and click the **Subscribe** button to add it.
4. **Wait for Synchronization:** The company will now appear under **My Companies**. The sync will start automatically the first time. Wait a few moments for it to complete.
5. **Confirmation of Sync:** After the sync is done, you will receive a confirmation message. Your Tally ledgers are now synced, and you can begin uploading documents and processing data.

## 5. How to Buy a Suvit Subscription
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upgrade or purchase a Suvit subscription?
* What payment methods are accepted?
* How long does account activation take after payment?

**Answer:**
You can purchase a Suvit Subscription directly through the website to access premium features. 

**Steps to Purchase:**
1. **Login:** Log in to Suvit. Go to **My Profile**, click on **Subscription & Billing**, and select **Buy Now**.
2. **Billing Info:** Fill in your billing details, add your Pincode, click **Save**, and then click on **Make a Payment**.
3. **Payment Page:** Enter your registered phone number and E-mail ID, then click **Continue**.
4. **Payment Gateway:** Choose your desired mode of payment and click **Continue**. 

Once your payment is complete through the portal, your subscription activates immediately *(Note: Payments made through custom payment links can take a few hours to activate)*. 

**After Purchasing:**
* Check your Email ID for a Welcome Message.
* Your dedicated Support Number can be found under your profile.
* On the next business day, you will be assigned an Onboarding/Training team to help you.
* For any changes to your invoice, email `support@suvit.io` within 10 days of your purchase.
