# Suvit Help Center Dataset: Sales and Sales Return

## 1. Mandatory Things to Check Before Using the Sales/Sales Return Module
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* What are the mandatory formatting rules for my Sales Excel sheet?
* Why is my Sales or Sales Return Excel file failing to upload?
* How do I format the invoice numbers and headers in Excel for Suvit?

**Answer:**
Before uploading your Sales or Sales Return data (including export of goods, exempt sales, diff GST, etc.) via Excel, you must ensure it strictly follows these formatting rules:
1. **First Sheet Only:** Make sure all your sales data is in the first sheet of your Excel file.
2. **Headers Go on Top:** The first row should have column names like Date, Invoice Number, Amount, etc. Don't write anything above the column names.
3. **Clean Column Titles:** Do not use dots (.) or dollar signs ($) in your headers.
4. **Always Fill the Invoice Number:** No empty boxes in the Invoice Number column.
5. **Keep Same Invoice Numbers Together:** If one invoice has many items, sort by Invoice Number (A–Z) to keep them grouped.
6. **Don't Write NA or Not Applicable:** If a field like GST doesn't apply, leave the cell blank. Do not write "NA" or "none".
7. **Use Exact Date Format:** Dates must be strictly DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 27/05/1992).
8. **No Extra Totals:** Delete rows with "Grand Total" or any unnecessary notes.
9. **Keep It Short:** Limit data to a maximum of 10,000 rows per sheet.
10. **Add a Ledger Column:** Add a column for Sales Ledger or Purchase Ledger based on the GST rate.
11. **Set Up GST Ledgers in Tally:** Ensure CGST, SGST, and IGST ledgers are already created under Duties & Taxes in Tally.
12. **Format Your Data Nicely:** Use TEXT or General format for text columns. Use NUMBER format for amounts, rates, etc.
13. **Add a Column Called Particular:** This is for the Sales Account Ledger based on the GST rate (e.g., if GST is 18%, write the ledger name linked to 18% Sales).
14. **Save as Excel Workbook:** Save the file in `.xlsx` format.

## 2. Uploading Sales/Sales Return Data Through an Excel Sheet
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I upload my sales invoices using an Excel file?
* Where do I find the bulk upload option for sales data?
* What is the maximum file size for Excel uploads in Suvit?

**Answer:**
Suvit allows you to upload Sales and Sales Return data through an Excel sheet to automate tracking and syncing with Tally.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. **Go to Bulk Upload:** From the left menu, click on **Bulk Upload**. Then choose either **Sales** or **Sales Return** from the dropdown.
2. **Click Upload:** Click on the **Upload** button in the top right corner.
3. **Select Your File:** Click "Click to upload" to choose your file from the computer. Ensure the file size is under 30MB, not password-protected, dates are in DD/MM/YYYY format, and headers don't contain special symbols like `.` or `$`. Once selected, click **Upload**.
4. **Verify Upload:** Your file will appear in the list with its name, type, and transaction count. 
*(Pro Tip: Always sort by Invoice Number A-Z to avoid mapping issues and ensure your Sales Account Ledger is mapped per your GST rate).*

## 3. Auto Mapping Sales Excel Sheet Data with SUVIT Configuration
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How does auto-mapping work for sales Excel sheets?
* How do I map my Excel columns to Tally fields in Suvit?
* Can Suvit automatically calculate GST during upload?

**Answer:**
Auto-mapping automatically matches your Excel sheet columns to the correct Tally fields. Once mapped, Suvit remembers this configuration for future uploads.

**Steps for Auto-Mapping:**
1. **Field Mapping:** Match "Your Sheet Header" (Excel columns) to the "Tally Fields". "Your Sheet Data" will show sample values to help you verify. Use the Configuration window to map extra details like Voucher Date, Cost Centres, Lading No., etc. Click **Next**.
2. **GST Mapping (Tax Ledger):** 
   * *Select from Tally:* Choose your Tally GST Ledger from the dropdown.
   * *Select from Excel:* Match the column from Excel where the GST Ledger Name is written.
   * *GST Auto Calculation:* Set to "Yes" if you want Suvit to calculate SGST, CGST, and IGST based on Tally settings. Set to "No" to pick manual values from the Excel sheet.
3. **Ledger Mapping:** Link remaining columns like Freight Amount, Discounts, and Round-off. Click **Save & Proceed**.

## 4. How Do We Process or Push Sales/Sales Return Data to Tally?
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I send my mapped sales data to Tally?
* What do the orange triangles mean on the processing screen?
* How do I bulk update ledgers or fix mismatches before pushing?

**Answer:**
After mapping your Excel sheet, you will land on the Processing Screen to review, fix, and push your data to Tally.

**Step-by-Step to Send to Tally:**
1. Select your transactions using the checkboxes (all or one by one).
2. Click the **Save** button.
3. Click **Send to Tally** and hit "OK" when prompted. *(Note: Only selected and saved data will be pushed).*

**Progress Tracking:**
* **Gray Stage:** Process has been initiated.
* **Orange Stage:** Progress has started.
* **Green Stage:** Data successfully sent to Tally.

**Fixing Mismatches (Orange Warning Triangles):**
An orange warning triangle indicates missing or unmapped details (like spelling mistakes or new party names). 
* **Create Missing Ledgers:** Click the "+" button next to a party name to add it.
* **Bulk Update:** Select multiple records and use the **Bulk Update** tool to change Voucher Types, Party Names, or Particulars all at once.

## 5. Create Ledger and Stock Items from Sales Transaction Screen
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I create a new party ledger or stock item directly from the Suvit sales screen?
* Can I create ledgers in bulk from the transaction screen?

**Answer:**
You can create party ledgers and stock items directly from the transaction screen in Suvit without having to switch back to Tally. Suvit syncs these new creations to Tally right before posting the entry.

**Method A: One-by-One Creation**
1. **Create Party Name (Ledger):** Click the **Create Ledger** ("+") button, fill in the party's details (Name, GST, etc.), and click **Add**.
2. **Create Stock Item:** Open the Stock Menu (drawer icon), click **Add a New Item**, fill in the item name, unit, and GST, and click **Add**.

**Method B: Bulk Creation**
Click on the **orange Plus Icon** on the screen to open the bulk creation window. This allows you to quickly add multiple Party Names or Stock Items at the same time.

## 6. OCR - Uploading and Processing Sales Bills
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I upload a sales invoice using an image or PDF?
* What is the file size limit for OCR sales uploads?
* How do I verify and sync scanned sales invoices?

**Answer:**
You can use Suvit's OCR feature to extract data from scanned PDFs or images of Sales invoices and map them to Tally.

**Uploading Steps:**
1. **Go to Transactions:** From the left menu, hover over **Sales** under the Transactions tab and click it.
2. **Upload File:** Click on **Upload Image** (top-right corner). Drag and drop or browse to select your files. *(Max 10 invoices at a time, up to 5 MB per file. Supported types: .pdf, .jpeg, .jpg, .png, .webp).* Click **Upload**.
3. **Verify Data:** Click on the uploaded invoice row to review the extracted data. 
   * Review if it is an "Item Invoice" or "Accounting Invoice".
   * Verify Voucher details, Customer Name, and GST.
   * Verify Item details (Quantity, Rate, Amount). Use the Configuration Window to enable extra fields like Item Narration.
4. **Tax & Finalization:** Add ledger amounts, select Tax components (SGST, CGST, IGST), review the totals, and add any narration.
5. **Save:** Click **Save & Close** to just save it, or **Save & Sync** to push it directly to Tally.
