## Part 1: Getting Started

### 1.1 How to Register in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I register for a new account on Suvit?
* What are the steps to create a Suvit profile?

**Answer:**
To register a new account on Suvit, follow these steps:
1. **Visit Website:** Go to the Suvit Website.
2. **Enter Mobile Number:** Enter your mobile number and click on **GET OTP**. Enter the received OTP and click **Sign In**.
3. **Fill Personal Information:** Enter your First Name, Last Name, and select your Profession. Click **Next**.
4. **Organization Information:** Enter the number of employees and indicate how you found out about Suvit. Click **Next**.
5. **Set Password:** Enter your E-Mail ID and set a secure password (minimum 8 characters). Click **Next**.

* *Image 1-6:* Progression of sign-up screens from mobile entry, OTP verification, personal/organization details, to password creation and the final welcome screen.

### 1.2 Verify your E-mail ID & Mobile Number
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I verify my email and mobile number?
* I didn't get the OTP for email verification.

**Answer:**
1. Go to Suvit.io and log in.
2. **Edit Profile:** Click on **My Profile**, then click **Verify**.
3. **Verify Details:** Enter the OTP sent to your contact details and click **Verify**. *(Check spam if email OTP is missing).*

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-2:* Dashboard showing "My Profile" -> "Verify" button and the OTP input modal.

### 1.3 Install Suvit Desktop (Tally Connector) Application
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I download and install the Suvit Tally Connector?

**Answer:**
1. **Download:** Log in to Suvit. Go to **Help & Resources** -> **Download**. (Downloads 64-bit by default; for 32-bit, email `support@suvit.io`).
2. **Install:** Double-click the downloaded file, click "Run", and wait for completion.
3. **Log In:** Open the app and enter your Suvit Username and Password.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-3:* "Help & Resources" download button, Windows Run prompt, and the Desktop App login screen.

### 1.4 Subscribe to the Company and Synchronize Ledgers
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I sync my Tally data with Suvit?

**Answer:**
1. **Open Tally:** Open your specific company in Tally.
2. **Add Company:** Open the Suvit Desktop App and navigate to **Add Companies**.
3. **Subscribe:** Find your company and click **Subscribe**.
4. **Sync:** The company will appear under **My Companies** and start syncing automatically. You will receive a confirmation message once done.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-5:* Tally open company view, Suvit app "Add Companies" list, the Subscribe button, sync progress bar, and success pop-up.

### 1.5 How to Buy a Suvit Subscription
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upgrade or purchase a Suvit subscription?

**Answer:**
1. Log in to Suvit -> **My Profile** -> **Subscription & Billing** -> **Buy Now**.
2. Fill in billing details, add Pincode, click **Save**, then **Make a Payment**.
3. Choose your payment method via the gateway. Accounts activate immediately through the portal.

* *Image 1-4:* Navigation path to billing, the billing form, contact confirmation, and the Razorpay gateway.

## Part 2: Banking Module

### 2.1 Import the Bank Statement
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I import a bank statement into Suvit?
* What file formats are not supported?

**Answer:**
1. **Navigate:** Sidebar -> **Bulk Upload** -> **Banking**.
2. **Choose Company:** Select your company from the top-right dropdown.
3. **Import:** Click **Import**, choose the Bank name, and upload the file (drag & drop).
4. **Wait:** Status changes from "Processing" to "Complete" or "Failed".
*Note: Passbooks, Share/Loan statements, and password-protected PDFs are NOT supported.*

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-8:* Navigation path, company dropdown, import button, bank selection modal, file upload zone, and processing status dashboard.

### 2.2 "Complete" & "Failed" Status
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * What do I do after it says "Complete"?
* Why did my bank statement upload fail?

**Answer:**
* **Complete:** Click the statement, select and save ledgers for transactions, and click **Send to Tally**.
* **Failed:** Check the failure reason. Common causes include missing column headers, merged alignment, closing balance mismatch, or password protection. Correct the file and re-upload.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-2:* The ledger selection screen and the "Send to Tally" action button. The Failed status dashboard showing error tooltips.

### 2.3 How to Select Ledger & Use Filters
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I bulk assign ledgers to transactions?

**Answer:**
* **Individual:** Use the dropdown at the end of each transaction row.
* **Bulk:** Search a keyword in the narration (e.g., "UPI"), select all checkboxes, and search/apply the Party Name in the LEDGER box.
* **Filters:** Use filters like Date, Amount, Narration, or "Hide Tally Pushed" to organize the view. Use Settings (⚙️) to enable Cost Centres or Bank Allocations.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-3:* Transaction table dropdowns, filter search bar with checkboxes, and the settings gear menu.

## Part 3: Sales and Sales Return

### 3.1 Mandatory Formatting for Sales Excel
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * What format should my Excel sheet be in for a sales upload?

**Answer:**
* Use the first sheet only, saved as `.xlsx`.
* Top row must contain headers (no dots `.` or dollar signs `$`).
* No empty invoice numbers; group items of the same invoice together.
* Dates must be DD/MM/YYYY. Max 10,000 rows.
* Include a "Particular" column for the Sales Ledger Name based on GST rate.

### 3.2 Uploading & Mapping Sales Data
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upload and map my sales invoices via Excel?

**Answer:**
1. **Upload:** Sidebar -> **Bulk Upload** -> **Sales**. Click **Upload**, select the `.xlsx` file.
2. **Map Fields:** Click the uploaded file. Match "Your Sheet Header" to "Tally Fields".
3. **GST Mapping:** Choose if Suvit should Auto-Calculate GST from Tally or pull manual values from your sheet. Save & Proceed.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-4:* Bulk upload navigation, file upload zone, Field Mapping UI, and GST Auto-Calculate toggle.

### 3.3 Process/Push Data & Create Ledgers
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I push sales to Tally or fix missing ledgers?

**Answer:**
* **Push:** Select transactions on the Processing Screen -> **Save** -> **Send to Tally**. Progress shows Gray (Initiated) -> Orange -> Green (Success).
* **Missing Ledgers (Orange Triangle):** Click the "+" next to a missing party name to create a ledger directly in Suvit, or use the **Bulk Update** button to assign ledgers to multiple rows at once.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-4:* Processing table with Send to Tally button, sync color indicators, the "+" ledger creation icon, and the Bulk Update modal.

### 3.4 Manual Invoice Creation
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Can I manually generate a sales invoice in Suvit?

**Answer:**
1. Dashboard -> **Create Bill** (Choose Sales or Return).
2. Toggle between **Accounting Invoice** (no items) or **Item Invoice** (with stock).
3. Fill Voucher Details, select Stock Items/Rates, apply Tax Ledgers.
4. Click **Save & Close** or **Save & Sync** (to push to Tally instantly).

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-6:* Create Bill button, Accounting vs Item toggle, manual data entry form, and the Save & Sync footer.

## Part 4: Purchase, Purchase Return & OCR

### 4.1 Purchase Excel Upload Formatting
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How should I format my purchase Excel sheet?

**Answer:**
*(The rules mirror Sales exactly)*. Data on the first sheet, strict DD/MM/YYYY date formatting, no blank invoice numbers, no special characters in headers, and an assigned Purchase Ledger column based on GST. Save as `.xlsx`.

### 4.2 OCR - Uploading Purchase Images/PDFs
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upload a scanned purchase invoice?

**Answer:**
1. Sidebar -> **Transactions** -> **Purchase**. Click **Upload Image** (Top right).
2. Upload a PDF, JPEG, or PNG (Max 5MB per file, up to 10 at a time).
3. Click the processed file to verify. Suvit auto-extracts data (50-80% efficiency initially). Verify Supplier, Items, Rates, and Tax Ledgers manually.
4. Click **Save & Sync**.

**[Image Context Reference]:**
* *Image 1-7:* Transaction menu, Upload Image button, drag-and-drop zone, OCR verification screen showing extracted data vs document, and the Save & Sync button.

## Part 5: Frequently Asked Questions

### 5.1 Unable to see Company/Ledger while uploading
**User Intent:** Why isn't my company/new ledger showing up?
**Answer:** Open the Suvit Desktop App, go to "My Companies", and click **Sync Master**. Wait for the success notification, then hard refresh (`CTRL + SHIFT + R`) the website.

### 5.2 Voucher Number Cannot be left blank
**User Intent:** My bank transactions failed to sync with this error.
**Answer:** In Tally, your Payment, Receipt, or Contra voucher types are set to Manual numbering. Go to `Master Alter` in Tally, change their numbering method strictly to **Automatic**, and re-sync.

### 5.3 Configure Tally Port with Suvit
**User Intent:** How do I connect the app to my Tally port?
**Answer:** 1. In Tally Prime, press `F1: Help` -> `Settings` -> `Connectivity`. Ensure ODBC is "Yes" and note the Port Number (e.g., 9050).
2. In the Suvit Desktop App `Settings`, set Tally Host to `localhost` and enter that exact Port Number. Save settings.

### 5.4 How to Subscribe a Split Company
**User Intent:** Does using a split company cost extra credits?
**Answer:** No, replacing an original with a split company is free. Open the split company in Tally, search for it in the Suvit App (it will say "Disabled"), click it to Enable, confirm, and Sync. *(Note: Working on BOTH original and split simultaneously uses 1 extra credit).*
