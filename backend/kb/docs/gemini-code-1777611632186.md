
# Suvit Help Center Dataset: OCR - Uploading and Processing Purchase Bills

## 1. Overview and Pre-requisites for OCR Purchase Uploads

**User Intent / Common Queries:**

* How do I upload a purchase invoice from an image or PDF in Suvit?
* What are the file size limits and supported formats for OCR?
* How accurate is the OCR feature for purchase bills?

**Answer:**
You can easily upload purchase invoices in Suvit using PDF or image files. The OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature automatically detects fields from the uploaded purchase invoice and matches them with your purchase details.

**⚠️ Important Notes & Limits:**

* **Batch Limit:** You can upload up to **10 invoices at a time**.
* **File Size Limit:** The maximum file size supported is **5 MB per file**.
* **Supported File Types:** `.pdf`, `.jpeg`, `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
* **Accuracy:** OCR efficiency for capturing purchase data is typically **50%–80%** during the initial entry, meaning you should always review and correct any errors manually before syncing to Tally.

## 2. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Upload the Invoice

**User Intent / Common Queries:**

* Where is the upload button for scanned purchase invoices?
* What are the exact steps to upload an image of a bill?

**Answer:**
To upload your scanned invoices or PDFs into Suvit, follow these steps:

1. **Go to Transactions Section:**
   * Click on the **Transactions** icon from the left-hand menu.
   * Hover over **Purchase** under the Transactions tab.
   * Click on **Purchase**.
2. **Click on Upload Image:**
   * Look at the top-right corner of your screen and click the **Upload Image** button.
3. **Upload the File:**
   * A pop-up window will appear. Either **drag & drop** your files into the designated area, or click **"Click to upload Image/PDF"** to browse your computer.
   * After the file successfully loads in the window, click the **Upload** button.
4. **Access the Uploaded Entry:**
   * After the upload processes, you will see a list of your invoices.
   * Click directly on the newly added invoice row to open the verification screen and edit the extracted data.

## 3. Step-by-Step Guide: Data Verification and Editing

**User Intent / Common Queries:**

* The OCR missed a line item, how do I add it?
* How do I change an OCR bill from an Item Invoice to an Accounting Invoice?
* How do I show the Supplier Invoice Number on the screen?

**Answer:**
Once the OCR has extracted the data, you must verify it against the visual preview of the document.

1. **Review and Select Invoice Type:**
   * Choose whether the purchase invoice data should be processed as **"With Item"** (Item Invoice) or an **"Accounting Invoice"** (Ledger only).
2. **Fill in / Verify Invoice Details:**
   * Check the **Voucher Type**, **Voucher Number**, and **Voucher Date**.
   * Verify the **Supplier Name** and **GST Number** (add additional supplier details if required).
   * Confirm the **Purchase Ledger Information**.
3. **Add or Edit Item Details (For Item Invoices):**
   * Ensure all purchased items are listed correctly.
   * Verify these fields: *Serial number, Item Name, Quantity, Rate, and Amount*.
   * If the OCR captured something incorrectly, you can edit the fields or delete any line item entirely.
4. **Use Configuration Options (Optional):**
   * If you need extra fields that are hidden by default, click the **Configuration Window** (⚙️ gear icon).
   * From here, you can enable fields like **Voucher Date**, **Supplier Invoice No.**, **Item Narration**, etc. Click Save once you are done adjusting your view.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide: Tax, Ledger, and Finalization

**User Intent / Common Queries:**

* How do I apply GST to my scanned purchase invoice?
* How do I finally send the OCR data to Tally?

**Answer:**
The final step is to ensure the taxes balance and send the verified entry to Tally.

1. **Edit Ledger Details:**
   * Scroll to the bottom to add or modify any additional purchase ledger names (like Freight or Discount) and their respective amounts.
2. **Add Tax Ledger Details:**
   * Enter the appropriate tax components such as **SGST**, **CGST**, and **IGST** along with their descriptions and exact amounts as per the physical bill.
3. **Review Narration and Totals:**
   * Add any necessary narration notes for your accounting records.
   * Double-check the **Subtotal**, **Tax Amount**, and **Total Amount** to ensure they match the original invoice exactly.
4. **Save and Synchronize:**
   * **Save & Close:** Click this if you want to save the purchase invoice in Suvit to review later, and close the current screen.
   * **Save & Sync:** Click this to save the invoice and instantly push the entry directly to Tally. *(Important: Ensure your Suvit Tally Connector desktop application is open and running during synchronization, otherwise it will fail to sync).*
