# Suvit Help Center Dataset: Sales and Sales Return

## 1. Mandatory Things to Check Before Using the Sales/Sales Return Module
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * What format should my Excel sheet be in for a sales upload?
* Why is my sales Excel file giving errors?
* What are the mandatory rules for preparing sales data in Suvit?

**Answer:**
Before uploading sales or sales return vouchers (including export of goods, diff GST, exempt sales, deemed export, etc.), you must ensure your Excel sheet is correctly formatted. 

**Mandatory Checklist:**
1. **First Sheet Only:** Ensure all your sales data is in the first sheet of your Excel file.
2. **Top Headers:** The first row must contain column names (e.g., Date, Invoice Number, Amount). Do not write anything above these column names.
3. **Clean Titles:** Do not use dots (.) or dollar signs ($) in your headers.
4. **Mandatory Invoice Numbers:** Do not leave empty boxes in the Invoice Number column.
5. **Group by Invoice:** If one invoice has multiple items, sort by Invoice Number (A–Z) to group them together.
6. **No 'NA' or 'None':** If a field (like GST) doesn't apply, leave the cell blank. Do not write "NA" or "Not Applicable."
7. **Date Format:** Dates must strictly be in DD/MM/YYYY format (e.g., 27/05/1992).
8. **No Extra Totals:** Delete rows with "Grand Total" or notes that are not needed for Tally.
9. **Row Limit:** Add a maximum of 10,000 rows per sheet.
10. **Sales/Purchase Ledger Column:** Add a column for Sales Ledger or Purchase Ledger based on the GST rate.
11. **Tally GST Ledgers:** Ensure CGST, SGST, and IGST ledgers are already created under *Duties & Taxes* in Tally.
12. **Formatting:** Use TEXT or General format for text columns, and NUMBER format for amounts/rates.
13. **Particulars Column:** Add a column named "Particular" to assign the Sales Account Ledger (e.g., if GST is 18%, write the ledger name linked to 18% Sales).
14. **Save Format:** Save your file specifically in Excel Workbook format (`.xlsx`).

## 2. Uploading Sales/Sales Return Data Through an Excel Sheet
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upload my sales invoices via Excel?
* Where is the bulk upload option for sales returns?
* What file size is allowed for sales uploads?

**Answer:**
Suvit allows you to upload sales data via Excel to automate tracking and syncing with Tally. 

**Step-by-Step Upload Process:**
1. **Go to Bulk Upload:** From the left menu under Data Entry Automation, click on **Bulk Upload**. Choose either **Sales** or **Sales Return** from the dropdown.
2. **Click Upload:** Click on the **Upload** button in the top right corner.
3. **Select Your File:** Click "Click to upload" to browse your computer.
   * *Prerequisites:* File must be under 30MB, not password-protected, dates in DD/MM/YYYY, headers free of '.' or '$', and ledgers synced.
4. **File Processing:** Click **Upload**. Your file will appear in the list with its name, type, and transaction count, confirming success.

*💡 Pro Tip: Double-check that the Sales Account Ledger is mapped to your GST rate. Always sort the file by Invoice Number A-Z to avoid mapping issues.*

## 3. Auto Mapping Sales Excel Sheet Data with SUVIT Configuration
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I match my Excel columns with Tally fields?
* What is Auto-Mapping in Suvit?
* How does Suvit calculate GST during Excel upload?

**Answer:**
Auto-mapping automatically matches your Excel sheet columns to the correct Tally fields. Once mapped, Suvit remembers your configuration for future uploads.

**Mapping Steps:**
1. **Field Mapping:** Match "Your Sheet Header" (Excel columns) to the respective "Tally Fields". The "Your Sheet Data" section shows sample values to help you verify. Use the Configuration window (⚙️) to map Voucher Date/Number, Party Name, Cost Centres, Dispatch Details, etc. Click Next.
2. **GST Mapping (Tax Ledger):** * *Select from Tally:* Choose your Tally GST Ledger from a dropdown.
   * *Select from Excel:* Match the column from Excel where the GST Ledger Name is written.
   * *GST Auto Calculation:* If set to "Yes", Suvit calculates SGST, CGST, and IGST automatically from Tally settings. If "No", it picks the manual values directly from your Excel sheet. *(Note: SGST, CGST, and IGST Tax Ledgers must be mapped).*
3. **Ledger Mapping:** Link remaining columns like Freight Amount, Discounts, and Round-off. 
4. Click **Save & Proceed** to move to the processing screen.

## 4. How Do We Process or Push Sales/Sales Return Data to Tally?
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I send my sales data from Suvit to Tally?
* What do the orange triangles mean on the processing screen?
* How can I bulk update party names before pushing to Tally?

**Answer:**
After mapping, you will land on the Processing Screen to review, edit, and push your data to Tally.

**Step-by-Step: Save & Send to Tally:**
1. Select your transactions (using bulk selection or individually).
2. Click **Save**.
3. Click **Send to Tally** and hit "OK" to confirm. *(Warning: Only selected/saved data will be sent).*

**Progress Tracking:**
* **Gray Stage:** Process has been initiated.
* **Orange Stage:** Progress has started.
* **Green Stage:** Data successfully sent to Tally.

**Fixing Mismatches (Orange Warning Triangles):**
If you see an orange warning triangle, it means data is missing (e.g., spelling mistake or new party name).
* **Bulk Check & Update:** Select the affected records, click **Bulk Update**, and apply the correct Voucher Type, Party Name, or Particulars (Ledger) to all selected rows at once.
* **Create Missing Ledgers:** Click the "+" button next to an unrecognized party name to instantly create a new ledger.

*💡 Pro Tip: Use General Filters to hide data already pushed to Tally, so you only focus on pending or failed records.*

## 5. Create Ledger and Stock Items from Sales Transaction Screen
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I add a new customer (party) while processing sales?
* Can I create stock items directly from Suvit without going to Tally?
* How do I bulk create missing ledgers?

**Answer:**
If an uploaded Excel sheet contains Party Names or Stock Items that don't exist in Tally yet, you don't need to leave Suvit. You can create them directly from the Transaction Screen.

**Method A: Single Entry Creation (One-by-One)**
1. **Create Party Name (Ledger):** Click the **Create Ledger** button (or the "+" icon next to a missing party). Fill in the name, GST details, etc., and click **Add**.
2. **Create Stock Item:** Open the Stock Menu by clicking the drawer icon. Click **Create Item**, fill in the item name, unit, and GST details, and click **Add**.

**Method B: Bulk Creation (All at Once)**
Click on the **orange Plus Icon** located on the screen to open the bulk creation window. This allows you to quickly add multiple Party Names or Stock Items simultaneously.

*💡 Pro Tip: Once you save the new ledgers/items in Suvit and click "Send to Tally" for that transaction, Suvit will automatically create the master ledger/item in Tally right before posting the entry!*

## 6. Create Invoice for Sales and Sales-Return
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Can I manually create a sales invoice in Suvit?
* How do I generate an item invoice vs an accounting invoice?
* How do I add taxes and narrations to a manual bill?

**Answer:**
You can manually generate Sales and Sales Return invoices directly within Suvit. 

**Steps to Create an Invoice:**
1. **Go to Dashboard:** Click "Explore Now" under Data Entry Automation.
2. **Create Bill:** In the Transaction Menu, click **Create Bill**.
3. **Choose Invoice Type:** Select either **Accounting Invoice** (without stock items) or **Item Invoice** (with stock items).
4. **Fill Details:** Enter the Voucher Type, Voucher No, Date, and Party Name. (GST details and configurations sync automatically from Tally).
5. **Add Items (Item Invoice Only):** Pick stock items from the dropdown, then enter the Quantity and Rate. You can continuously add multiple items.
6. **Taxes & Narration:** Select Tax Ledgers (SGST, CGST, IGST) and add any optional narration. Verify the Taxable and Total Amount.
7. **Save the Bill:** Click **Save & Close** to finish, or **Save & Sync** to instantly push the newly created invoice to Tally.

*(Note: Fields marked with an asterisk `*` are mandatory and cannot be skipped. If you need to add things like Supplier Invoice No. or Item Narration, use the Configuration (⚙️) window to enable those fields).*
