## 1. I am unable to see the company or ledger while uploading the document
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Why isn't my company showing up when I try to upload a document?
* I created a new ledger in Tally, but it's not showing in Suvit.
* How do I sync a newly assigned company?

**Answer:**
If you cannot see or select a company/ledger while uploading a document, it is usually due to a sync issue. This happens if you have newly subscribed to a company or if a secondary user has just been assigned to it. 

**Steps to Sync the Company:**
1. **Open Suvit Application:** Go to the "My Companies" tab in the Suvit Desktop Application and click on the **Sync Master** button.
2. **Wait for Confirmation:** Wait for the notification on your screen showing "Ledger Sync Successfully."
3. **Refresh Website:** Go back to the Suvit website and perform a hard refresh by pressing `CTRL + SHIFT + R`. You should now be able to try your import/upload again.

*(Note: Whenever you create or alter a new ledger/item in Tally, simply click "Sync Master" from the Suvit Tally Connector to fetch the latest data).*

## 2. Voucher Number Cannot be left blank (Banking)
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Why am I getting a "Voucher Number cannot be left blank" error?
* My banking transactions failed to sync to Tally.
* How do I fix manual voucher numbering errors in Tally?

**Answer:**
Bank entries in Suvit are posted under Payment, Receipt, or Contra vouchers. If any of these voucher types in Tally use "Manual" or "Automatic (Manual Override)" numbering instead of purely "Automatic", Tally will reject the sync and throw the error: *"Voucher Number cannot be left blank."*

**Steps to Resolve:**
1. **Check Exception Report (Tally Prime V3+):** Press `Alt+Y` (Data) -> `All Exceptions`. Clear or delete all exception entries related to this error.
2. **Access Voucher Settings in Tally:** Go to `Master Alter` in Tally. *(Important: Take a backup of your company before making changes).*
3. **Set to Automatic:** Find and open the **Payment**, **Receipt**, and **Contra** voucher types. Change their voucher numbering methods strictly to **Automatic** and save.
4. **Re-Sync:** Open the Suvit application, sync the data again, and push the failed transactions back to Tally.

## 3. How to check Tally port or change port in Tally Prime
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I find my Tally Prime port number?
* How do I enable ODBC in Tally Prime?
* What port should I use for Suvit?

**Answer:**
If you are using Suvit on multiple systems with multiple Tally instances, each Tally ODBC port must be unique. 

**Steps to Check/Change Tally Port:**
1. **Open Help Menu:** In Tally Prime, press `F1: Help` to access the Configuration menu. Select **Settings**, then click on **Connectivity**.
2. **Client/Server Configuration:** Navigate to **Client/Server Configuration** and press Enter.
3. **Configure ODBC:** * Set "Tally Prime Act as" to **Both** (to enable local and remote access).
   * Enable ODBC by setting it to **Yes**.
   * Set the Port Number (default is usually `9050`, but you can choose any number between `9000` to `9999`).
4. **Restart Tally:** Select **Yes** when prompted to restart Tally Prime to apply the changes.

Once Tally restarts, open your company and configure this exact port number in the Suvit Desktop Application.

## 4. How to configure Tally port with Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I connect the Suvit Desktop app to my Tally port?
* Suvit is not connecting to Tally, how do I match the port?

**Answer:**
Tally port configuration is crucial for connectivity between Suvit and Tally. You must ensure the port in Suvit matches the ODBC port set in Tally.

**Steps to Configure:**
1. **Access Desktop App:** Open the Suvit Desktop application and log in.
2. **Go to Settings:** Navigate to the **Settings** menu within the desktop app.
3. **Set Port:** Set the Tally Host to `localhost`. Enter the exact same Tally port number that is configured in your Tally Prime software (e.g., `9050`).
4. **Save:** Click on **Save Settings** to apply the changes and establish the connection.

## 5. How to Subscribe Split Company in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Does splitting my company in Tally cost extra credits in Suvit?
* How do I use a split Tally company in Suvit?
* Why is my newly split company showing as disconnected?

**Answer:**
After splitting a subscribed company in Tally, Suvit allows you to subscribe to the split version in place of the original *without* using any additional credits. *(Note: If you want to actively work on BOTH the Main Company and Split Company simultaneously, an additional credit will be utilized).*

**Steps to Subscribe to a Split Company:**
1. **Open Split Company:** Launch Tally and open the newly split company.
2. **Open Suvit Application:** Log into the Suvit Desktop App. *(Your previous company will show as disconnected).*
3. **Search Company:** Go to the **Company** tab in the menu and search for your split company's name.
4. **Enable Company:** The split company status will initially show as "Disabled". Click on the company to activate/enable it.
5. **Confirm:** Click **OK** on the subscription confirmation pop-up. 
6. **Sync:** Run a sync from the Suvit application. Your split company is now ready to use without extra credit consumption.

*(Important: Once a Split company is subscribed, the credit cannot be reversed or exchanged under any condition).*
