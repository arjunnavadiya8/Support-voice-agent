# Suvit Knowledge Base — Getting Started

**Module Overview:**
New to Suvit? This module provides step-by-step guidelines to set up your Suvit account efficiently and manage your accounting automation process.

---

## 1. How to Register in Suvit

Suvit is an accounting automation platform. You can create an account by visiting the Suvit website at https://in.suvit.io/signUp.

### Step 1: Visit the Website
Go to https://in.suvit.io/signUp

### Step 2: Enter Mobile Number
- Enter your mobile number.
- Click on **GET OTP** — you will receive an OTP on your number.
- Enter the OTP and click **Sign In**.

### Step 3: Fill Personal Information
- Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
- Select your **Profession**.
- Click **Next**.

### Step 4: Organization Information
- Enter **Number of Employees or Staff**.
- Answer: How did you find out about us?
- Click **Next**.

### Step 5: Set Your Password
- Enter your **E-Mail ID**.
- Set your **Password** (minimum 8 characters).
- Click **Next**.

### Step 6: Registration Complete
Your registration is done successfully. You will now be taken to your Suvit dashboard.

---

## 2. Verify Your E-mail ID & Mobile Number

After registration, it is important to verify your email and mobile number for smooth profile setup.

### Steps:
1. Go to https://www.suvit.io and log in.
2. Click on **My Profile**.
3. Click on **Verify**.
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your email or mobile.
5. Click the **Verify** button.

> **Note:** If you didn't receive the OTP in your email inbox, check your **spam folder**.

### Why Is Verification Important?
Every Suvit user has a profile with their name, mobile number, GSTIN, address (optional), and email ID. Keeping this updated ensures your account works seamlessly.

### What's Next?
Once email and mobile verification are done, you're ready to install the Tally Connector Desktop application.

---

## 3. Install Suvit Desktop (Tally Connector) Application

The Suvit Connector acts as a bridge between your Tally software and Suvit, ensuring seamless data processing.

### Important Notes Before Installing:
- By default, the 64-bit application downloads automatically.
- To check if your system is 32-bit or 64-bit: check under System Properties in Windows.
- For the 32-bit Suvit Desktop Application, email: support@suvit.io to receive the download link.

### Step 1: Download the Connector
1. Login to Suvit at https://in.suvit.io/signIn
2. Go to **Help & Resources**.
3. Click on **Download**.

### Step 2: Installation
- Double-click the downloaded application file.
- Click **"Run"** if prompted.
- Wait for the installation to complete.

### Step 3: Log In to the Connector
- Open the installed Suvit Desktop Application.
- Enter your Suvit **Username** (Mobile number or Email ID) and **Password**.
- Follow any on-screen instructions to configure Tally with Suvit after successful login.

---

## 4. Subscribe to the Company and Synchronize the Ledgers

To process your data in Tally, the company needs to be subscribed in Suvit and its ledgers must be synced.

### What Suvit Automates:
- Bank Statement
- Sales and Sales Return
- Purchase and Purchase Return
- Journal Entries
- Bulk Party/Stock Item Creation
- Client and User Management

### Step 1: Open the Company in Tally
- Open the company you want to work with in Tally and make sure it is selected.

### Step 2: Add the Company in Suvit Connector
- Open the **Suvit Desktop Application** (find it on your desktop).
- Go to **"Add Companies"**.

### Step 3: Subscribe the Company
- Click the **Subscribe** button next to the company you want to add.

### Step 4: Wait for Synchronization
- Once subscribed, it will appear under **"My Companies"**.
- The sync will start automatically for the first time. Wait a few moments.

### Step 5: Confirmation of Sync
- After sync is done, you'll see a **confirmation message**.
- Your Tally ledgers are now synced with Suvit.
- You can now upload documents in desired modules and process data to Tally.

## 5. How to Buy a Suvit Subscription

Suvit Subscription can be purchased directly through the website.

### Step 1: Login to Suvit
1. Login at https://in.suvit.io/signIn
2. Go to **My Profile**.
3. Click on **Subscription & Billing**.
4. Click on **Buy Now**.

### Step 2: Complete Billing Info
- Add your **Pincode**.
- Click **Save**.
- Click on **Make a Payment**.

### Step 3: Payment Page
- Enter your **Registered Phone number & Email ID**.
- Click **Continue**.

### Step 4: Payment Gateway
- Choose your desired **Mode of Payment**.
- Click **Continue**.
- Once payment is complete, your subscription will **activate immediately**.
  *(Payment made through any link can take a few hours to activate your account.)*

### Welcome to Suvit — After Purchase:
- Check your Email for the Welcome Message.
- Support Number is given under your profile.
- Next business day, you will be assigned an Onboarding/Training team.
- For any changes in Invoice, email support@suvit.io within **10 days** of purchasing.

### What Can You Do With Your Suvit Subscription?
1. **Upload Unlimited Documents** — Add all your important documents.
2. **Banking Module** — Process payments, receipts, and other transactions easily.
3. **Sales/Purchase Module** — Handle sales, purchases, and receipts.
4. **Bulk Master Creation** — Create many stock items and ledgers at once.
5. **Client Management** — Give clients a Suvit ID to upload their documents.
6. **Manage Your Team** — Assign tasks to staff using Suvit.
7. **Beta Program** — Get early access to upcoming features.
