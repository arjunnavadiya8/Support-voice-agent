# Suvit Help Center Dataset: Banking Module

## 1. Import the Bank Statement
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I import a bank statement into Suvit?
* What are the steps to upload bank statements to Tally?
* How long does it take to process a bank statement?
* What file formats are not supported for bank uploads?

**Answer:**
To import and process a bank statement from Suvit to Tally, follow these steps:
1. **Sign In:** Visit Suvit, enter your Email and Password, and click **Sign In**.
2. **Navigate to Banking:** From the sidebar under "Data Entry Automation," click on **Bulk Upload** and choose **Banking** from the dropdown.
3. **Choose the Company:** Click the top-right corner dropdown or use the search bar to find and select your company.
4. **Import Statement:** Click the **Import** button located in the top-right section.
5. **Select Bank:** From the pop-up window, choose the relevant Bank (e.g., SBI Bank, UCO Bank).
6. **Upload the File:** Drag and drop your file or click to upload. Ensure the correct bank and file are selected, then click the **Upload** button. *(Note: A warning will appear if a duplicate file name exists).*
7. **Processing Status:** The file will appear in the "Processing" state. Wait until the status changes to "Complete" or "Failed". Refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R) if it takes too long.

**Document Processing Times:**
* Excel Sheet: Up to 30 minutes
* Original PDF: Up to 1 hour
* Scanned PDF: Up to 12 hours

**Unsupported File Types:**
Passbooks, Share Market Statements, Loan Statements, Password-Protected PDFs, RTP/TXT formats, and Dot Matrix font formats are *not* supported.

## 2. "Complete" Status After Processing the Statement
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * What should I do after my bank statement shows "Complete"?
* How do I send processed bank data to Tally?
* How do I assign ledgers after the statement is processed?

**Answer:**
When the statement status appears as "Complete," it has been successfully processed. You can now select ledgers and send the data to Tally.

1. **Select and Save Ledgers:** Click on the completed statement to open the extracted data. You can select ledgers individually or use filters to select them in bulk. Once assigned, click save.
2. **Send Transactions to Tally:** Ensure both Tally and the Suvit Desktop Application are open. Select the saved transactions and click the option to send them to Tally.
3. **Verify in Tally:** Once successfully sent, open Tally to verify that the entries have been synchronized correctly.

## 3. "Failed" Status After Processing the Statement
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Why did my bank statement upload fail?
* How do I fix a failed bank statement?
* What are the formatting rules for bank statements?

**Answer:**
If your uploaded bank statement changes from "Processing" to "Failed," you will need to check the failure reason, correct the document, and re-upload it. 

**Common Reasons for Failure:**
* Unsupported formats or password-protected files.
* Closing Balance mismatch.
* Missing column headers.
* Handwritten, pen/ink marks, or punching marks on the statement.

**Tips to Avoid Document Failure:**
* **Proper Alignment:** Ensure data is not merged (e.g., dates shouldn't merge with narrations, narrations shouldn't overflow into amounts).
* **Complete Data:** Ensure no transactions are missing and that they follow chronological order.
* **Column Headers:** Statements *must* include headers like Date, Narration, Debit Amount, and Credit Amount on every page/part.
* **Scanned PDFs:** Must be aligned, not too blurry or dark, free from ink/punch marks, and without cropped data.
* **Excel Sheets:** Data must be on the first sheet of the workbook with proper column headers.

## 4. How to Select Ledger?
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I assign a ledger to a transaction?
* How can I select ledgers in bulk for similar transactions?
* How do I handle Contra entries in Suvit?

**Answer:**
Once a bank statement is successfully uploaded, you can assign ledgers to your transactions either individually or in bulk. 

**Method 1: Choose Specific Ledgers Individually**
Select the appropriate ledger for each transaction one by one from the "Select Ledger" dropdown box available at the end of the transaction row.

**Method 2: Select Ledgers in Bulk**
Use filters to assign ledgers to similar transactions all at once.
1. Search for a common keyword in the narration (e.g., "charges", "neft", "upi").
2. Select all the filtered transactions using the checkboxes.
3. Search for the desired Party Name in the LEDGER box and select it to apply it to all selected rows.

**Handling Contra Entries:**
Click on the "Payment" or "Receipt" label beside a transaction. A drop-down menu will appear. Select "Contra Withdraw" or "Contra Deposit" as per your requirement and save. (You can also change transaction types in bulk using the "Transaction Type" bulk operation).

*Note: If a ledger doesn't exist in Tally yet, you can create it directly from Suvit.*

## 5. Use of General Filters
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * What filters can I use to sort my bank transactions in Suvit?
* How do I hide transactions that are already sent to Tally?
* How do I configure bank allocation or cost centers?

**Answer:**
Suvit provides various filters to simplify the ledger selection process and specific configurations for advanced tracking.

**Available Filters:**
1. **Date-Filter:** Filter by a specific date range. (You can delete irrelevant dates from Suvit if they don't need to go to Tally).
2. **Narration/Description Filter:** Search common keywords (e.g., "G-pay") to bulk-assign ledgers.
3. **Payment/Receipt Filter:** Isolate specific transaction types.
4. **Amount Filter:** Find transactions within a certain monetary range.
5. **Hide Tally Pushed:** Hides transactions already sent to Tally so you can focus on pending ones.
6. **Blank Records:** Displays transactions that do not have a ledger selected yet.
7. **Saved Records:** Lists transactions with ledgers assigned but not yet sent to Tally.
8. **Unsaved Records:** Views transactions where ledgers were selected but the save action wasn't completed.

**Configurations (Accessed via Settings -> Configuration):**
* **Bank Allocation:** Maps bank accounts accurately for ledger entries.
* **Cheque/Instrument Number:** Records check numbers or transaction IDs for better tracking.
* **Supplier/Reference Number:** Records supplier invoice numbers for purchases.
* **Cost Centre Tracking:** Allocates expenses/revenues to specific departments or projects.
* **Voucher Numbering:** Manages the sequence of entries (manual or automatic) in Tally.
*Click "Save" after making configuration changes.*
