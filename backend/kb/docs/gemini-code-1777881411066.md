# Suvit Help Center Dataset: OCR - Uploading and Processing Sales Bills

## 1. Overview and Pre-requisites for OCR Sales Uploads
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I upload a sales invoice from an image or PDF in Suvit?
* What are the file size limits and supported formats for Sales OCR?
* How accurate is the OCR feature for sales bills?

**Answer:**
You can easily upload Sales invoices in Suvit using PDF or image files. The OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature automatically detects fields from the uploaded sales invoice and matches them with your sales details in Tally. 

**⚠️ Important Notes & System Limits:**
* **Batch Limit:** You can upload up to **10 invoices at a time**.
* **File Size Limit:** The maximum file size supported is **5 MB per file**.
* **Supported File Types:** `.pdf`, `.jpeg`, `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
* **Accuracy:** OCR efficiency for capturing sales data is typically **50%–80%** during the initial entry. Because of this, fields will be automatically detected, but you must manually review and correct any errors before syncing.

## 2. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Upload the Sales Invoice
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* Where is the upload button for scanned sales invoices?
* What are the exact steps to upload an image of a sales bill?

**Answer:**
To upload your scanned invoices or PDFs into Suvit, follow these steps:

1. **Go to Transactions Section:** 
   * Click on the **Transactions** icon from the left-hand menu.
   * Hover over **Sales** under the Transactions tab.
   * Click on **Sales**.
2. **Click on Upload Image:** 
   * Look at the top-right corner of your screen and click the **Upload Image** button.
3. **Upload the File:** 
   * A pop-up window will appear. Either **drag & drop** your files into the designated area, or click **"Click to upload Image/PDF"** to browse your computer.
   * After the file successfully loads in the window, click the **Upload** button.
4. **Access the Uploaded Entry:**
   * After the upload processes, you will see a list of your invoices.
   * Click directly on the invoice row to open the verification screen to edit and verify the extracted data.

## 3. Step-by-Step Guide: Data Verification and Editing
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I change an OCR sales bill from an Item Invoice to an Accounting Invoice?
* The OCR missed an item on my sales bill, how do I fix it?
* How do I show the Customer Invoice Number on the screen?

**Answer:**
Once the OCR has extracted the data, you must select your invoice type and verify the details against the visual preview of the document.

1. **Review and Select Invoice Type:** 
   * Choose whether the sales invoice data should be processed as **"With Item"** (Item Invoice) or an **"Accounting Invoice"** (Ledger only).
2. **Fill in / Verify Invoice Details:** 
   * Check the **Voucher Type**, **Voucher Number**, and **Voucher Date**.
   * Verify the **Customer Name** and **GST Number** (add additional customer details if required).
   * Confirm the **Sales Ledger Information**.
3. **Add or Edit Item Details (For Item Invoices):** 
   * Ensure all sold items are listed correctly in the table.
   * Verify these fields: *Serial number, Item name, Quantity, Rate, and Amount*.
   * You can edit any field manually or delete any incorrect line item if needed.
4. **Use Configuration Options (Optional):**
   * If you need extra fields that are hidden by default, click the **Configuration Window** (⚙️ gear icon).
   * From here, you can select additional fields to display during voucher entry, such as **Voucher Date**, **Customer Invoice No.**, and **Item Narration**. 
   * Click Save once you are done making changes.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide: Tax, Ledger, and Finalization
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I apply GST to my scanned sales invoice?
* How do I finally send the OCR sales data to Tally?

**Answer:**
The final step is to ensure the ledger amounts balance, the taxes are correct, and the entry is synchronized to Tally.

1. **Edit Ledger Details:** 
   * Scroll down to add or edit any additional sales ledger names (like Freight or Discount) and their respective amounts.
2. **Add Tax Ledger Details:** 
   * Add the required tax components such as **SGST**, **CGST**, and **IGST** along with their descriptions and exact amounts as per the physical bill.
3. **Review Narration and Totals:** 
   * Enter any additional notes in the **Narration** field.
   * Double-check the **Subtotal**, **Tax Amount**, and **Total Amount** to ensure they match the original scanned invoice.
4. **Save and Synchronize:**
   * **Save & Close:** Click this if you want to save the sales invoice in Suvit for later review and close the current screen.
   * **Save & Sync:** Click this to save the invoice and instantly sync it directly with Tally. *(Important: Ensure your Suvit Tally Connector desktop application is open and running during synchronization, otherwise it will fail to sync).*
