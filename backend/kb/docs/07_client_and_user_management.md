# Suvit Knowledge Base — Client and User Management Module

**Module Overview:**
A client and user can be easily managed in Suvit itself. This module covers creating user accounts, defining roles and permissions, creating client accounts, reviewing secondary user reports, and tracking activity logs.

---

## 1. How to Create a User Account in Suvit

You can manage your work efficiently by creating user accounts and providing them with specific access. You can easily add team members or staff to Suvit and assign specific roles and company access.

### Steps to Add a New User:

#### Step 1: Go to the User & Roles Section
- Go to **User & Roles** section from the left-hand menu.

#### Step 2: Click the "+ Add User" Button
- Click the **"+ Add User"** button on the top right.

#### Step 3: Fill in the Required Details
- First Name, Last Name
- Email ID, Mobile Number
- Create a Password

#### Step 4: Select the Role
- Select the **Role** you want to assign to the user (e.g., Data Entry Operator, Manager).

#### Step 5: Select Company Access
- Keep this part to assign **all companies**, or select from the dropdown to provide **limited company access**.

#### Step 6: Click "Add User" to Complete
- Click **"Add User"** to finish the process.

### Important Notes:
- Each user must have a **unique email ID**.
- Make sure the user's role matches their job responsibility.
- You can edit, delete, or manage access of users anytime from the User & Roles section.

### Use Case Examples:
- Give **data entry staff** access only to Data Entry Automation.
- Give **senior team members** full access to all companies and features.

## 2. How to Create Personalized Roles in Suvit

You can create multiple roles with the help of Role Management and manage the modules your Users can work in.

### Step 1: Go to Role Management
- Sign in to Suvit with the **Primary account** (Main Account).
- Click on **Role Management** from the left menu.
- Click on **"Create New Role"**.

### Step 2: Create a New Role
- On the Role Management page, click on **Create New Role**.

### Step 3: Name the Role and Set Permissions
- Provide a **Role Name** based on your requirements.
- Enabling **Full Access** grants complete control over all permissions — including viewing, creating, editing, and deleting data.
- **Note:** If you give full access using this option, the user who gets this role can add and subscribe to companies.

### Step 4: Select the Role Type
On the Create Roles page, you will have two default role types:
- **Data-Entry Automation**
- **GST Automation**

Select the role type based on your requirement and click **Create Role**.

### Step 5: Role Created Successfully
- Click **"Create Role"**.
- Upon successful creation, a confirmation message will appear: **"Role Created Successfully"**.

### Step 6: Assign Roles to Users
- Now you are ready to assign Roles to your Users.
- Go to User & Roles → select the user → assign the role.

> **Note:** Update the user role system whenever a new feature is added to Suvit.

## 3. How to Create a Client Account in Suvit

You can manage your client documents efficiently by creating accounts for your clients and providing them with specific access.

### Step 1: Go to Clients Management
- Sign in to Suvit with the **Primary account** (Main Account).
- Click on **Client** from the left menu.
- Click on **"Add Client"**.

### Step 2: Fill in Client Details
- **First Name / Last Name:** Enter your client name.
- **Email / Mobile:** Ensure the client's email ID and mobile number are unique; the number should be active on WhatsApp.
- **Password:** You can keep the system-generated password, or type your desired password (any combination of letters, numbers, and symbols).

### Step 3: Assign Company
- Select the companies you want to assign to your client.
- You can select either a single company or multiple companies as needed.

### Step 4: Add the Client
- Once all details are entered, click **"Add Client"**.

### Step 5: Share Client Credentials
- A confirmation message **"Client Added Successfully"** will appear.
- Click on **Copy Info** and share the username and password with your client.
- Now your client is ready to use Suvit with their credentials.

### What Clients Can Do After Getting Access:
- Upload their documents (bank statements, sales/purchase data) directly.
- All uploaded documents will show up automatically in the accountant's Suvit account for processing.

## 4. How to Access and Review Secondary User Reports in Suvit

Suvit's "Secondary User Reports" feature lets you monitor activities of secondary users easily. Track user actions and maintain control from a central dashboard.

### Step 1: Sign In to Suvit with the Primary Account
- Sign in to Suvit using your **Primary Account** (Main Account).

### Step 2: Access the User Report Tab
- Click on **Users** from the left menu.
- Within the "Users" section, find the tab labeled **"User Report"**.
- Click on **"User Report"**.

### Step 3: Explore Module-Wise Secondary User Reports
- You will be directed to a page with secondary user reports in a **module-wise** fashion.
- The reports provide a detailed breakdown of activities performed by secondary users.
- Data available includes:
  - Transactions
  - Uploads
  - Saves
  - Synchronization with Tally

### What You Can Monitor:
- Which user uploaded which document
- Which user processed or saved which transaction
- Which user sent data to Tally
- Time and date stamps for all activities

## 5. Suvit Activity Log — How It Works

You can access the Activity Log to view all activities that have been done on the Suvit platform by any user.

### Step 1: Sign In to Suvit with the Primary Account
- Sign in to Suvit with the **Primary account** (Main Account).

### Step 2: Open Profile
- Click on the **Profile** icon/link (at the top right).

### Step 3: Activity Log
- Within the profile, find the option labeled **Activity Log**.
- Go to: https://in.suvit.io/activity-log

### What the Activity Log Shows:
Each record in the Activity Log includes:
- **Unique ID** — Unique identifier for each action
- **Timestamp** — Exact date and time of the action
- **User** — Which user performed the action
- **Company** — Which company was involved
- **Module** — Which module was used (e.g., Banking, Sales, Purchase)
- **Sub-Module** — Which sub-section within the module
- **Action Performed** — What action was taken (upload, save, send to Tally, etc.)
- **Related Documents** — Which document was involved

### Purpose of the Activity Log:
- Monitor and track your recent actions.
- Efficiently manage and keep records of activities for both your account and user accounts.
- Ensure accountability and security across your team.
- Investigate any discrepancies or unauthorized changes.

## Key Quick Reference for Voice Agent

**Q: How do I add a new team member/staff to Suvit?**
A: Go to User & Roles → click "+ Add User" → fill in name, email, mobile, password → select Role → select Company access → click "Add User".

**Q: How do I create a new role in Suvit?**
A: Go to Role Management → click "Create New Role" → enter role name → set permissions → select role type (Data Entry Automation or GST Automation) → click "Create Role".

**Q: What is the difference between a User and a Client in Suvit?**
A: A **User** is a team member/staff who works within the accountant's Suvit account. A **Client** is an external client who gets their own login and uploads documents which then appear in the accountant's account.

**Q: How do I add a client to Suvit?**
A: Go to Client from left menu → click "Add Client" → fill name, email, mobile, password → assign companies → click "Add Client" → copy credentials and share with client.

**Q: How do I track what my staff is doing in Suvit?**
A: Go to Users → click "User Report" tab to see module-wise activities of secondary users. Or check the Activity Log under Profile for detailed timestamped records.

**Q: What information does the Activity Log provide?**
A: Unique ID, timestamp, user name, company, module, sub-module, action performed, and related documents.

**Q: Can I give a user access to only specific companies?**
A: Yes. When adding a user, you can select specific companies from the dropdown instead of giving access to all companies.

**Q: Can I give a client access to multiple companies?**
A: Yes. When creating a client account, you can select either a single company or multiple companies to assign to them.

**Q: What are the two types of roles available in Suvit?**
A: Data-Entry Automation and GST Automation. You can also create custom roles with specific permissions.

**Q: Where do I find the Activity Log?**
A: Go to Profile → Activity Log, or directly visit https://in.suvit.io/activity-log
