## 1. How to Create a User Account in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I add my team members or staff to Suvit?
* How do I create a user account and assign them specific companies?
* Can I restrict a staff member's access to only certain companies?

**Answer:**
You can manage your work efficiently by creating user accounts for your team and providing them with specific access. You can easily add staff members to Suvit and assign specific roles and company access.

**Steps to Add a New User:**
1. **Navigate:** Go to the **User & Roles** section from the left-hand menu.
2. **Add User:** Click the **+ Add User** button located on the top right.
3. **Fill Details:** Enter the required details:
   * First Name & Last Name
   * Email ID & Mobile Number
   * Create a Password
4. **Assign Role:** Select the appropriate Role you want to assign to the user (e.g., Data-Entry Automation or GST Automation).
5. **Assign Company:** Select the company/companies you want to give access to. You can select "All Companies" or choose specific companies from the drop-down menu to provide limited access.
6. **Finish:** Click on **Add User** to complete the process.

**🔐 Important Notes:**
* Each user must have a unique Email ID.
* Make sure the user's role matches their job responsibility.
* You can edit, delete, or manage the access of users anytime from the User & Roles section.

## 2. How to Create a Client Account in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I give my clients access to view their own documents?
* How do I create an account for a client?
* How do I share Suvit login credentials with a client?

**Answer:**
You can manage your client documents efficiently by creating accounts specifically for your clients, allowing them to access their own data securely.

**Client Management: Creating a New Account**
1. **Go to Clients Management:** Sign in to Suvit using the Primary account (Main Account). Click on **Client** from the left menu, then click on the **Add Client** button.
2. **Fill in Client Details:** 
   * **First Name/Last Name:** Enter your Client's name.
   * **Email/Mobile:** Ensure the client's Mail ID and Mobile Number are unique. (The number should be active on WhatsApp).
   * **Password:** You can keep the system-generated password, or you can type a custom password (any combination of letters, numbers, and symbols).
3. **Assign Company:** Select the companies you want to assign to your client. You have the option to select either a single company or multiple companies as needed.
4. **Add the Client:** Once all details are entered, click the **Add Client** button.
5. **Share Client Credentials:** A confirmation message stating *"Client Added Successfully"* will appear. Click on **Copy Info** and share the username and password with your client. Your client is now ready to use Suvit with their credentials.

## 3. How to Create Personalized Roles in Suvit
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I set custom permissions for my staff?
* What does "Full Access" mean in role management?
* How do I restrict users from adding or subscribing to new companies?

**Answer:**
You can create custom roles with specific permissions tailored to your team's responsibilities. 

**Role Management Guide:**
1. **Go to Role Management:** Sign in to Suvit with the Primary account. Click on **Role Management** from the left menu, then click on **Create New Role**.
2. **Name the Role and Set Permissions:** Provide a Role Name based on your requirements. 
   * *Important Note on Full Access:* Enabling **Full Access** grants complete control over all permissions, including viewing, creating, editing, and deleting data. Users with full access can also add, manage, and subscribe to companies without restrictions. Keep this disabled if you want to limit their control.
3. **Select the Role Type:** On the Create Roles page, you will choose from two default role types based on your requirement:
   * **Data-Entry Automation**
   * **GST Automation**
4. **Create Role:** Choose the Role Type and click **Create Role**. Upon successful creation, a confirmation message *"Role Created Successfully"* will appear.
5. **Assign Roles to Users:** You are now ready to assign this newly created role to your users from the User & Roles section.

## 4. Suvit Activity Log - How It Works
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I check what my team has been doing in Suvit?
* Where is the audit trail or history of actions?
* Can I track which user uploaded or deleted a specific document?

**Answer:**
You can access the Activity Log to view a comprehensive record of all actions performed on the Suvit platform. This ensures effective tracking, monitoring, and security for your workspace.

**Managing and Reviewing Account Activities:**
1. **Sign In:** Log in to Suvit using the Primary Account (Main Account).
2. **Open Profile:** Click on your **Profile** icon (usually at the top right).
3. **Access Activity Log:** Within the profile menu, click on the **Activity Log** link.
4. **Review Data:** On the Activity Log page, you can view a detailed record of user actions. The log includes:
   * Unique ID
   * Timestamp (Date & Time)
   * User Name
   * Company Name
   * Module & Sub-module used
   * Action performed (e.g., uploaded, saved, deleted)
   * Related documents

## 5. How to Access and Review Secondary User Reports
**User Intent / Common Queries:** 
* How do I monitor the productivity of my staff?
* Where can I see a report of how many transactions my secondary users processed?
* How do I track team performance module-wise?

**Answer:**
Suvit's "Secondary User Reports" feature lets you easily monitor the activities and productivity of your secondary users (staff) from a central dashboard.

**Accessing Secondary User Reports:**
1. **Sign In:** Sign in to Suvit using your Primary Account (Main Account).
2. **Access the User Report Tab:** Click on **Users** from the left menu. Within the "Users" section, locate and click on the tab labeled **User Report**.
3. **Explore Module-Wise Reports:** Upon clicking the tab, you will be directed to a page where you can explore secondary user reports categorized by module. 
   * *Details Provided:* This report gives a detailed breakdown of activities performed by secondary users, including data related to **transactions processed, uploads completed, saves made, and successful synchronizations with Tally.**

By reviewing these reports, you gain a comprehensive understanding of your team's contributions, helping to enhance accountability and workflow efficiency within your workspace.
