# Suvit Help Center Dataset: General Overview, Banking, Sales, Purchase & OCR

## 1. Getting Started with Suvit
*   **Registration & Account Setup:** Visit [in.suvit.io/signUp](https://in.suvit.io/signUp), enter your mobile number, verify via OTP, and fill in personal and organization details.
*   **Account Verification:** Log in, navigate to "My Profile", and verify both your email and mobile number using OTP verification.
*   **Tally Connector Installation:** Download the 64-bit Suvit Desktop application from the "Help & Resources" section, run the installer, and log in with your Suvit credentials.
*   **Company Subscription & Sync:** Open your company in Tally, add it in the Suvit Desktop App, and click "Subscribe". Wait for the ledger synchronization to complete.
*   **Purchasing Subscription:** Go to "Subscription & Billing" in your profile, complete your billing info (pincode), and pay via the secure gateway.

## 2. Banking Module & Reconciliations
*   **Importing Bank Statements:** Navigate to "Bulk Upload > Banking", select your company and bank ledger, and upload the statement in PDF or Excel format.
*   **Processing Transactions:** Wait for the status to show "Complete". Then select ledgers for each transaction (individually or in bulk) and click "Save" before sending them to Tally.
*   **Filtering Entries:** Use Narration, Date, Amount, and Transaction Type filters to quickly categorize entries.

## 3. Sales, Purchase, and OCR Processing
*   **Excel Upload Checklist:** Ensure your data is on the first sheet, headers are in the first row, invoice numbers are present, and dates are in `DD/MM/YYYY` format.
*   **OCR for Sales (Images & PDFs):** Go to "Transactions > Sales", click "Upload Image", select your files, and Suvit will extract data (Date, Invoice No, Party, Amount) for your review.
*   **Ledger & Stock Item Creation:** Missing ledgers or stock items can be created directly from the transaction screen, either individually or in bulk.
*   **OCR for Purchase (Images & PDFs):** To upload scanned purchase invoices or PDFs into Suvit, follow these steps:
    - Click on the Transactions icon from the left-hand menu.
    - Hover over Purchase under the Transactions tab and click on Purchase.
    - Click on Upload Image from the top right-right corner.
    - Drag & drop your files into the designated area or click to browse.
    - Click the Upload button. After the upload processes, you will see a list of your invoices. Click directly on the invoice row to open the verification screen to edit and verify the extracted data.

## 4. FAQs, Connectivity, & Settings
*   **Tally Port Configuration:** If connectivity fails, go to "Settings" in the Suvit Desktop App and ensure the "Tally Port" (default 9000) matches your Tally software configuration.
*   **Common Errors:** Ensure all ledgers are synchronized before attempting to upload Sales or Purchase data to avoid "Ledger not found" errors.
