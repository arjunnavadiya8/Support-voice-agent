# Suvit Help Center Dataset: Purchase and Purchase Return

## 1. Mandatory Things to Check Before Using Purchase/Purchase Return Module
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How should I format my Excel sheet for a purchase upload?
* What are the mandatory rules for purchase data in Suvit?
* Why is my purchase return Excel file failing?

**Answer:**
Before uploading your Purchase or Purchase Return Excel file into Suvit, you must ensure it is neat, clean, and formatted correctly for Tally.

**Checklist for Excel Sheet:**
1. **First Sheet Only:** Keep your data in the first worksheet of your Excel file.
2. **Top Headers:** The first row should have titles like Date, Invoice No., GST, Amount, etc. Do not put extra rows or headings above your data.
3. **No Special Characters in Headings:** Do not use symbols like `.` or `$` in your column titles.
4. **Mandatory Invoice Numbers:** No blank invoice numbers are allowed.
5. **Group Same Invoice Numbers:** Sort your data A–Z by Invoice Number to keep multi-item invoices lined up properly.
6. **Avoid 'NA' or 'Not Applicable':** If a value like GST isn't available, just leave the cell empty. Do not write "NA", "none", or "not applicable".
7. **Date Format:** Dates must strictly use the DD/MM/YYYY format (e.g., 27/05/2022).
8. **Delete Extra Totals/Notes:** Remove rows like “Grand Total” as Tally doesn't need them.
9. **Row Limit:** Keep your file under 10,000 transactions/rows per sheet.
10. **Add a Ledger Column Based on GST:** Create a column that contains the Purchase Ledger Name as per the GST rate.
11. **GST Ledgers Must Exist in Tally:** Ensure SGST, CGST, and IGST are created under *Duties & Taxes* in your Tally company.
12. **Correct Excel Formats:** Set text fields to *TEXT* or *General* format. Set numbers to *Number* format.
13. **Add "Particular" Column:** Fill this with the correct Purchase Ledger Name based on the GST rate (e.g., If GST is 18%, use the ledger defined for 18% Purchase).
14. **Save Format:** Save the file strictly as an "Excel Workbook" (`.xlsx`) format—not CSV or PDF.

## 2. Uploading Purchase/Purchase Return Data Through an Excel Sheet
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * How do I upload my purchase invoices via Excel?
* Where is the bulk upload option for purchase returns?
* What happens to my original Excel file when I upload it to Suvit?

**Answer:**
You can load your purchase data into Suvit to automate tracking. When you upload the data, Suvit creates a new worksheet mapping, while your original purchase data worksheet stays exactly the same.

**Step-by-Step Upload Process:**
1. **Navigate to Module:** From the left menu, click on **Data Entry Automation**, then go to **Bulk Upload** -> **Purchase** or **Purchase Return**.
2. **Upload File:** Click the **Upload File** button (an upload pop-up screen will appear).
3. **Select File:** Click "Click to Upload" to browse and select the Excel file from your computer system. Click **Upload** to finish adding the document.
4. **Access the Mapping Screen:** After the upload is successful, the file will appear in your list. Click on the file name to proceed to the Data Mapping screen.

## 3. Create Invoice for Purchase and Purchase-Return
**User Intent / Common Queries:** * Can I manually enter a purchase bill in Suvit?
* How do I add stock items to a manual purchase invoice?
* How do I sync a manually created purchase return to Tally?

**Answer:**
You can manually create Purchase and Purchase Return bills directly in Suvit, ensuring accurate data entry and smooth syncing with Tally.

**Steps to Create an Invoice:**
1. **Go to Dashboard:** Click on **Data Entry Automation** -> **Transaction** -> **Purchase/Purchase Return**.
2. **Create Bill:** Click the **Create Bill** button and choose whether it is a Purchase or a Purchase Return.
3. **Invoice Details (Toggle Accounting vs. Item Invoice):**
   * **Voucher Details:** Enter the transaction type, Invoice No., Date, Party (Supplier Name) & GST, and choose the Purchase Ledger account.
   * **Item Details (For Item Invoices):** Use the fields to list the Sr. No., Item Name, Qty, Rate, and Amount. 
   * **Ledger Details (For Accounting Invoices):** Fill in the Ledger Name and Amount for non-stock expenses.
4. **Customer/Supplier Details:** The sidebar will display supplier information (name, phone, address, credit terms, and outstanding payables) automatically synced from Tally.
5. **Configuration Options:** Click the **Configuration Window (⚙️)** to show/hide fields like Voucher Date, Supplier Invoice No., or Item Narration. Save changes once done.
6. **Tax, Narration, and Totals:** Select and apply taxes, add necessary notes/narrations, and verify the final amounts.
7. **Save the Bill:** * Click **Save & Close** to save and exit.
   * Click **Save & Sync** to immediately push the bill to Tally.

*(Note: Fields marked with "*" are mandatory. If a transaction fails to sync, check the `tally.imp` file in your Tally installation folder).*
